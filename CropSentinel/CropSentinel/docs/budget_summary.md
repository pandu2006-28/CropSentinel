## Procurement rules
- Confirm exact models, compatibility, current prices, shipping, taxes and lead times.
- The ₹4,000 logistics line is an allowance, not a quote.
- The ₹3,500 contingency is held unspent unless needed.
- The ₹5,000 spares bucket is an allocation; it does not require all spares to be purchased on day one.
- If verified prices exceed budget, reduce optional expenses or revisit scope rather than inventing prices.
